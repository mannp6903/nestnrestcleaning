Questions

Cleaning in Wexford: questions.

Anything else? Email nestnrestcleaning@gmail.com.

How is my price calculated?

Your price is based on the service, the number of bedrooms and bathrooms, the type of home and any extras you add. You see it before you book. If your home needs much more or less time than expected, we tell you before we start, never after.

Do I pay when I book?

No. There's no deposit. You pay after the clean by Interac e-Transfer, credit or debit card, or cash.

What if something is missed?

Tell us within 24 hours and we'll come back and re-clean those areas, free of charge.

Do I need to be home?

No. You can leave access with a concierge, lockbox or key code. Choose how we get in when you book and we'll confirm the details by text.

Can I reschedule or cancel?

Yes. Let us know at least 24 hours before your arrival window and there's no charge.

Which areas do you serve?

Scarborough and North York. Enter your postal code on the site to check. If you're just outside these areas, get in touch and we'll tell you honestly whether we can help.

How do I get in touch?

Email nestnrestcleaning@gmail.com, or send your details through the booking form. Mann replies within 2 hours during business hours (every day, 9am to 11pm).
