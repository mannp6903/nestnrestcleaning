Questions

Gift card questions.

Anything else? Call or text 647 978 6903, or message on WhatsApp.

Do gift cards expire?

No. Nest & Rest gift cards never expire, and they can be used toward any service. Any balance left after a clean stays on the card.

How do I pay for a gift card?

Send the form and we'll text you to confirm the details and send an Interac e-Transfer request. Your gift card is emailed as soon as it's paid.

How does the person I'm gifting book?

They book online, by text or on WhatsApp like anyone else and mention the code on their gift card. We take the gift value off their price after the clean.

Can the gift be for someone outside Scarborough or North York?

The clean has to be in Scarborough or North York. If you're not sure, enter their postal code on the gift page to check.
