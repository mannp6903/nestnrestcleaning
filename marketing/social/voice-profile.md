# Voice profile: Mann Patel, Nest & Rest Cleaning

Built: 2026-10-31 · Sources: Website copy written in Mann's voice (vision letter, FAQs, host page) and the README message templates
Updated: 2026-10-31: initial. Confirm or correct it, and every future caption follows.

| Trait | How Mann writes |
|---|---|
| Greeting | "Hi Priya, it's Mann from Nest & Rest Cleaning." First name, then who he is. |
| Sign-off | "— Mann" in messages; "Mann, Nest & Rest Cleaning" where the business needs naming. |
| Sentences | Short to medium, about 12 words. Plain words. One idea per sentence. |
| Contractions | Yes: I'll, you'll, it's, don't, I'd. |
| Exclamations | Rare. Warmth comes from the words, not the punctuation. |
| Calls it | a clean, your clean, a deep clean, a regular plan, turnovers, the standard, founding households, request a clean |
| Formality | First names, no titles. Warm and plain, like talking to a neighbour. |

## Characteristic phrases
- "We clean. You rest."
- "Home is for rest."
- "the same careful way every visit"
- "your price before you book"
- "nothing to pay until the clean is done"
- "I clean every home myself"
- "Saturday goes to the bathrooms and the floors"

## Never uses
- best in Toronto, #1, top-rated (no reviews yet to back it)
- insured, bonded, eco-friendly, background-checked (not true yet: the site keeps these off until they are)
- limited time, selling fast, spots filling up
- Most popular (unless booking records show it)
- Exciting news, thrilled to announce, solutions, hassle-free
- before-and-after photos that aren't from your own jobs, or reviews you didn't receive
