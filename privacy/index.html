Privacy Policy

This policy explains what personal information Nest & Rest Cleaning collects, why, who it is shared with, and the choices you have. We follow Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA).

1. Who we are

Nest & Rest Cleaning is a home cleaning business owned and operated by Mann Patel in Scarborough, Toronto, Ontario. Mann Patel is responsible for how we handle personal information and is our privacy contact. You can reach us at nestnrestcleaning@gmail.com. Our mailing address is available on request.

2. What we collect

WhenWhat we collect
You request a bookingName, mobile number, email (optional), street address and postal code, home type and size, extras, preferred day and arrival window, how we get in (for example a lockbox or concierge), pets, notes, and how you'd like us to confirm.
You ask us to text you a priceYour mobile number and the price details you chose.
You request a quoteName, phone, email (optional), postal code, details about the space or property, and any notes you add.
You request a gift cardYour name, phone and email, the gift amount, the recipient's name, and optionally their email, postal code and your message.
You leave a reviewRating, first name and last initial, neighbourhood (optional), the service, your review, and the phone or email you booked with so we can match it to your visit.
You call, text, WhatsApp or email usYour number or address and what you tell us.
You ask about working with usYour name, contact details and what you tell us about your experience.
You payPayment records such as the amount, date and method. We do not store full card numbers.
You visit the websiteTechnical information your browser sends, such as your IP address and browser type, which our website host receives to deliver the site.

We only collect what we need to arrange and deliver your clean. Please don't send us sensitive information we haven't asked for, such as health or financial details.

3. How we use it

  • To schedule, confirm and deliver your cleaning, and to get into your home as you've arranged.
  • To contact you about your booking, quote, gift card, a change of plans or a problem with a visit.
  • To send you a confirmation email when you give us your email address with a request.
  • To prepare quotes, keep payment and tax records, and meet our legal obligations.
  • To publish your review on our website, using only your first name, last initial, neighbourhood and review, if you've sent us one.
  • To improve our service, for example by learning which services clients book.
  • To consider you for work, if you've asked about working with us.

We do not sell or rent your personal information, and we don't use it for any purpose not listed here without asking you first.

4. Your consent

When you send us a booking request, price request, quote request, gift card request or review, you agree to us using your information for the purposes above. You can withdraw your consent at any time by contacting us, subject to legal or contractual limits. If you do, we may not be able to provide some services.

We send you messages about your booking, quote or gift card, and occasionally ask for a review after a clean. When you ask us to text you a price, we text it once. We won't send you promotional emails or texts without your consent, and every message we send will tell you how to opt out. If you ask us to stop, we'll stop within 10 business days, as Canada's Anti-Spam Legislation (CASL) requires.

5. Who we share it with

We share personal information only with service providers who help us run the business, and only what they need:

  • GitHub (GitHub, Inc.) hosts this website and keeps standard server logs, including IP addresses, for security.
  • Google provides our email service, where booking, quote, gift card and review messages are stored.
  • Google Analytics (Google LLC) measures how visitors use this website: the pages viewed, how they arrived (for example from our flyer or business card), the type of device and an approximate location. It doesn't receive anything you type into our forms, and it stores data in the United States.
  • FormSubmit (formsubmit.co) delivers the website's forms to our email inbox, and sends your confirmation email when you give us your address.
  • Your bank or card payment provider processes payments you make by Interac e-Transfer or card.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it, for example in response to a valid court order.

6. Storage outside Canada

Some of our service providers store or process information outside Canada, including in the United States. While it is there, it is subject to the laws of that country and may be accessed by its courts, law enforcement and national security authorities. We choose established providers and share only what each one needs.

7. How long we keep it

  • Access codes and key instructions: deleted within 7 days of your last scheduled visit, or sooner if you ask.
  • Client and booking records: kept while you're a client, then for as long as tax law requires (generally six years for financial records).
  • Price and quote requests that don't become bookings: deleted within 12 months.
  • Job enquiries: deleted within 12 months if we don't hire you, or sooner if you ask.
  • Gift card records: kept until the gift card is used up, then for as long as tax law requires.
  • Reviews: kept while published. Ask us and we'll remove yours.

8. How we protect it

Only the people who need your information to serve you can see it. We protect our email and business accounts with strong passwords and two-step verification, keep access codes separate from other records, and delete information we no longer need. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect your information.

If a breach of our safeguards creates a real risk of significant harm to you, we will notify you and the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada as the law requires.

9. Door and lockbox codes

Codes and keys give access to your home, so we treat them with extra care. We don't ask for them on the website. We ask by text or phone only after your booking is confirmed, keep them separate from your name and address, never write them on keys, and delete them within 7 days of your last scheduled visit. We recommend changing your code from time to time, and after you stop using our service.

10. Cookies and your browser

We don't use advertising cookies or tracking pixels. We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the site, and it sets its own cookies in your browser (their names start with "_ga"). We've turned off Google's advertising features for this site. To opt out, block cookies in your browser settings or install Google's opt-out add-on at tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout. When you start a booking, the details you enter are saved in your own browser so you don't lose them if you leave the page. They stay on your device and are sent to us only when you submit the request. You can clear them at any time through your browser settings.

If we add advertising tools in future, we will update this policy first and explain how to opt out.

11. Your rights

You can ask to see the personal information we hold about you, ask how it has been used or shared, and ask us to correct it if it's wrong. Contact us at nestnrestcleaning@gmail.com. We'll respond within 30 days, and there's no charge.

12. Children

Our services and website are for adults. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children.

13. Questions and complaints

If you have a concern about how we handle your information, please contact Mann Patel first at nestnrestcleaning@gmail.com. We'll look into it and reply.

You can also complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada at priv.gc.ca, by phone at 1-800-282-1376, or by mail at 30 Victoria Street, Gatineau, Quebec K1A 1H3.

14. Changes to this policy

We'll update this policy when our practices change and show the new effective date at the top of this page.
