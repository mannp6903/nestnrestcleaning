How much does house cleaning cost in Toronto?

By Mann Patel, founder of Nest & Rest Cleaning

Most Toronto cleaning companies price a visit by the size of the home, the type of clean and how often you book. Here's what moves the number, with our own prices as worked examples, so you can compare any quote with confidence.

The five things that set the price

  1. Size. Bedrooms and bathrooms are the best predictor of time on site. Bathrooms take the longest per square foot, so each extra bathroom adds more than you might expect.
  2. Type of home. A townhouse or house has stairs, more floor space and more entrances than a condo with the same bedrooms. We add 10% for a townhouse and 20% for a house.
  3. Type of clean. A regular clean keeps a home right. A deep clean adds the detail work that builds up over months, and a move-out clean covers an empty home inside every cabinet and closet.
  4. How often. Regular visits cost less each time, because the home never falls far behind. Our plans save 10% every 4 weeks, 15% every 2 weeks and 20% weekly.
  5. Extras. Inside the oven or fridge, interior windows and laundry are usually priced separately, so you only pay for what you need.

Hourly or flat price?

Some companies charge by the hour, others quote a flat price. Hourly can look cheaper until the clean runs long. A flat price, set from your home's size before you book, tells you the total up front. Ours is flat: if your home needs much more or less time than expected, we tell you and agree the price before we start, never after.

How to compare two quotes fairly

  • Is tax included? Many companies quote before 13% HST. Ask whether the number is the final number.
  • Are supplies included? Products, cloths, mops and a vacuum should be part of the price.
  • What does the clean include? Compare the room-by-room list, not just the name of the service.
  • Is there a deposit? And what does it cost to cancel or reschedule?
  • What if something is missed? A free re-clean within a day or two is a good sign.

Our answers: no HST is added today, supplies are included, every service has a room-by-room list, there's no deposit, changes are free up to 24 hours before, and if we miss a spot, tell us within 24 hours and we'll re-clean it free.
