The Nest & Rest standard

Five things happen at your door. Every visit.

You should never wonder who is in your home, or what they carried in with them. So every visit begins and ends the same way.

  1. Ring once, introduce by name

    You'll know who is coming before we arrive.

  2. Coat onto our own hanger

    It hangs on our kit, never in your closet.

  3. Street shoes off, house shoes on

    Your floors only ever meet clean soles.

  4. Apron tied, gloves on

    One apron for kitchens, one for baths. Never both in one room.

  5. Leave in reverse

    Gloves off, apron folded, shoes bagged. Nothing left behind.

Questions

Questions, answered.

Anything else? Call or text 647 978 6903.

How is my price calculated?

Your price is based on the service, the number of bedrooms and bathrooms, the type of home and any extras you add. You see it before you book. If your home needs much more or less time than expected, we tell you before we start, never after.

Do I pay when I book?

No. There's no deposit. You pay after the clean by Interac e-Transfer, credit or debit card, or cash.

What if something is missed?

Tell us within 24 hours and we'll come back and re-clean those areas, free of charge.

Do you bring supplies and equipment?

Yes. We bring cloths, mops, a vacuum and our products. If you'd like us to use your own products, or need fragrance-free, add it to your booking notes.

Do I need to be home?

No. Many clients leave access with a concierge, lockbox or key code. Choose how we get in when you book and we'll confirm the details by text.

Can I reschedule or cancel?

Yes. Text us at least 24 hours before your arrival window and there's no charge.

Are pets OK?

Absolutely. Tell us about them when you book. For heavy shedding, add the pet hair extra so we schedule enough time.

Why do you wear house shoes?

Street shoes carry grit and salt, especially in a Toronto winter. We change into indoor-only shoes at your door, so your floors only meet clean soles.

Which areas do you serve?

Scarborough and North York. Enter your postal code on the site to check. If you're just outside these areas, call or text and we'll tell you honestly whether we can help.
