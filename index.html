      How we work

      The first minute at your door.

      You should never have to wonder who is in your home or what they brought in with them. Every visit starts and ends the same way.

      Look for the navy uniform, the Nest & Rest logo patch and a first-name badge. That's us.

      1. Ring once, introduce by name

        You'll know who is coming before we arrive.

      2. Coat onto our own hanger

        It hangs on our kit, never in your closet.

      3. Street shoes off, house shoes on

        Your floors only meet soles that never go outside.

      4. Apron tied, gloves on

        One apron for kitchens, one for baths. Never both in one room.

      5. Leave in reverse

        Gloves off, apron folded, shoes bagged. Nothing left behind.

          Questions

          Good to know before you book.

          Do you bring your own supplies?

          Yes. We bring cloths, mops, a vacuum and our products. If you prefer we use your products, or need fragrance-free, just add it to your booking notes.

          Do I need to be home?

          No. Many clients leave access with the concierge, a lockbox or a key code. Pick how we get in when you book and we'll confirm the details by text.

          How long does a clean take?

          Your estimate shows the expected time. A regular clean of a 1-bedroom condo takes about 2 hours. Deep and move-out cleans take longer.

          When and how do I pay?

          After the clean. We accept Interac e-Transfer, credit or debit card, and cash. There's no deposit to book.

          What if I need to reschedule or cancel?

          Text us at least 24 hours before your arrival window and there's no charge. Late cancellations may be charged a fee to cover the booked time.

          What if I'm not happy with the clean?

          Tell us within 24 hours and we'll come back and re-clean the areas you're not happy with, free of charge.

          Are pets OK?

          Absolutely. Tell us about them when you book. For homes with heavy shedding, add the pet hair add-on so we schedule enough time.

          Is the price on the website final?

          It's an estimate based on what you tell us. If your home needs much more or less time than expected, we'll tell you before we start. You'll never get a surprise bill.
