Every turnover

What I do between guests.

The same checklist every time, so each guest walks into the same clean, reset unit.

  • Kitchen

    • Guests' dishes washed and put away
    • Counters, sink and taps cleaned
    • Stovetop, microwave and appliance fronts wiped
    • Fridge cleared of anything guests left
    • Floors vacuumed and mopped

  • Bathrooms

    • Toilet cleaned inside and out
    • Tub, shower and glass cleaned
    • Sink, counter and mirror cleaned
    • Floors washed

  • Bedrooms and living

    • Beds stripped and made
    • Surfaces and décor dusted
    • Under beds and sofa cushions checked for left items
    • Floors vacuumed and mopped

  • Whole unit

    • Bins emptied and bags taken out
    • Door handles, light switches and remotes wiped
    • Lights off, windows closed, door locked
    • A final walk-through before I leave

Host questions, answered.

Anything else? Email nestnrestcleaning@gmail.com.

Who will clean my unit?

Me. I'm Mann Patel, the owner, and I do every turnover myself, so your unit is cleaned the same careful way each time.

How do you get in if I'm not there?

With your lockbox, door code or smart lock. I ask for the code by text once a turnover is confirmed. I never copy keys or write codes on them, and I delete codes within 7 days of your last scheduled clean.

Can you do same-day turnovers?

Yes. I clean 7 days a week from 9am to 11pm. Tell me your usual checkout and check-in times, and I'll confirm I can fit each turnover in between before you rely on it.

How does payment work for my listings?

You pay after each turnover by Interac e-Transfer, credit or debit card, or cash. There's no deposit, and each property gets its own receipt.

What if something is missed?

Tell us within 24 hours and we'll come back and re-clean those areas, free of charge.

Can I reschedule or cancel?

Yes. Let us know at least 24 hours before your arrival window and there's no charge.

Which areas do you serve?

Scarborough and North York. Enter your postal code on the site to check. If you're just outside these areas, get in touch and we'll tell you honestly whether we can help.
