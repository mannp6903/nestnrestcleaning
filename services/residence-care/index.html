Residence Care

For homes over about 3,000 square feet, or homes with natural stone, marble, hardwood and glass that need the right product on the right surface. We start with a walkthrough, write a room-by-room care plan, and keep the same team on your home whenever possible.

  • In-person walkthrough before the first visit
  • Written room-by-room care plan
  • Surface-appropriate products for stone, marble, wood and glass
  • The same team each visit whenever possible
  • Flexible scheduling around your household
  • Discreet, uniformed, house-shoes-only service

What if something is missed?

Tell us within 24 hours and we'll come back and re-clean those areas, free of charge.

Why do you wear house shoes?

Street shoes carry grit and salt, especially in a Toronto winter. We change into indoor-only shoes at your door, so your floors only meet clean soles.

How long does a residence walkthrough take?

Usually 20 to 30 minutes. We walk each room with you, note surfaces and priorities, and send a written care plan and quote afterwards.
