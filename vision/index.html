Our vision

Home is for rest.

Nest & Rest exists to give families in Scarborough and North York their time back, and to set a new standard for the care that comes into your home. Here is what we believe, what we promise, and where we're going.

A note from Mann

For most families, a clean home is something you earn on the weekend. Saturday goes to the bathrooms and the floors, and rest waits until the list is done. Often, it never is.

I started Nest & Rest because I think that trade is backwards. Your home should give you rest, not take it away. Every hour spent scrubbing is an hour not spent with your kids, your parents, your friends or yourself.

So I take the list off your hands. I do it the same careful way every visit, so you never have to check, and I hold Nest & Rest to the promises on this page. I would rather grow slowly and keep them than grow quickly and break them.

Today, I clean every home myself. When there are a hundred of us, every clean will still carry my name.

Mann PatelFounder & Owner

What we believe

The ideas behind every visit.

  1. Rest is the real product.

    Cleaning is how we deliver it. We measure ourselves by the hours a family gets back, not the rooms we finish.

  2. Trust is earned at the door.

    Who walks into your home matters as much as what they do there. So every visit begins and ends the same way, and we publish exactly how.

  3. Care is a craft.

    Cleaning well is skilled work. The people who do it deserve proper training, the right tools, and to be introduced by name.

  4. Small promises, kept, become a reputation.

    Your price before you book. Payment after it's done. Every rating published. Kept every visit, these are worth more than any advertisement.

Our promises

What you can count on. What we'll never do.

A promise only counts if it's kept every time. Hold us to each of these. If we ever fall short, tell Mann directly.

Always

  • Your exact price before you book
  • Nothing to pay until the clean is done
  • You'll know who is coming, by name
  • A free re-clean if anything isn't right
  • Every rating published, not only the good ones

Never

  • A deposit to hold your booking
  • A price change after we start
  • Street shoes on your floors
  • Keys copied, or codes written on them
  • A paid, filtered or hidden review
