# Animate the nest, keep the engine

A world-class team would rebuild Nest & Rest's presentation layer around four things no competitor has: the "We clean. You rest." duality, the code-drawn Toronto-at-night landmark art, Mann as the owner who comes to your door, and the room-by-room checklist. It would keep the static, config-driven pricing and booking engine that already works. The world's best cleaning-service websites can't supply the "unique" part. On documented evidence the top ten (Hellamaid, Maid Marines, Homeaglow, Maid Sailors, Sparkly Maid NYC, AspenClean, TIDY, Clean Proper, The Maids and Custom Maids) win on instant pricing, booking speed and content that answer engines can quote. No residential cleaner turned up at Awwwards Site of the Day level, so the craft has to come from award winners in nearby categories: Sunday Robotics, Pest Stop Boys, Blueland and Matic. The motion should be calm, not busy: slow "settle" easing, a night-to-dawn scroll story on the landmark art, a route line drawn through the home, and a "build your clean" house that fills with rooms as the price updates. It should be built CSS-first, with about 48 KB of GSAP only on the pages that need choreography. Every word, price and JSON-LD block stays in static HTML, because the major AI crawlers don't run JavaScript. On GEO and AEO the opening is consistency. Every local competitor shows conflicting prices or review counts, so one dated price guide, pages that open with the answer, and identical guarantee wording would make Nest & Rest the most quotable cleaner in Scarborough and North York. The biggest risk isn't visual. The site has no phone number, no reviews and no Google Business Profile link, and AI answers to "who is the best cleaner" questions in Toronto mostly cite directories and review platforms. One caveat governs the ranking: the research environment blocked live access to every competitor site, so no site's visual and motion craft could be scored, and the international platforms are still unverified candidates.

### What the leaders share, and where they slip

**In the hero, the price is in the first screen or one click away.** Hellamaid pairs "See My Price & Book" with a call button and a live promo code ([Hellamaid](https://hellamaid.ca/house-cleaning-service-toronto/)). Maid Marines puts flat pricing and employed cleaners in its page title ([Maid Marines](https://maidmarines.com/cleaning-services-nyc)), and TIDY puts "from $55" in its title ([TIDY](https://www.tidy.com/services/house-cleaning)). "60 seconds" has become a shared claim. Handy says "book in 60 seconds" ([Handy](https://www.handy.com/)), Maid Sailors says "booking takes just 60 seconds" ([Maid Sailors](https://maidsailors.com/)), and Hellamaid titles a guide "Book a Cleaning with Hellamaid in 60 Seconds!" ([Hellamaid](https://hellamaid.ca/cleaning-guide/how-to-find-the-best-professional-house-cleaning-services-in-ontario/)). Speed alone therefore no longer sets anyone apart. The franchises still lead with an estimate request, as in Molly Maid's "Call for a Free Estimate!" ([Molly Maid](https://www.mollymaid.com/)). No published study compares calculator-first, postcode-first and estimate-first heroes for cleaning; the evidence is simply that the strongest independents have all converged on the calculator. Nest & Rest already leads with an instant price box, so its hero has to win on what speed can't copy: the owner at the door, the same cleaner every time, an all-in price and visible scope.

**On landing pages, the strongest AEO architecture belongs to Homeaglow.** It has a brand-query answer page ([/is-legit](https://www.homeaglow.com/is-legit)), comparison pages such as "The Maids Cleaning Service Reviews — Try Homeaglow Instead" ([/vs/the-maids](https://www.homeaglow.com/vs/the-maids)), a cost explainer and a gift-card hub ([gift cards](https://www.homeaglow.com/hub/gift-cards)). Maid Marines' borough → neighbourhood hierarchy (/brooklyn/flatlands, /brooklyn/sea-gate) is the model for Scarborough → Agincourt ([Maid Marines](https://maidmarines.com/brooklyn/sea-gate)). Clean Proper's Etobicoke page shows what genuinely local copy sounds like: lakeside towers with "concierge and fob systems… share your building's check-in details once" ([Clean Proper](https://cleanproper.ca/locations/etobicoke/)).

The mistakes teach as much. Maid Sailors' Herald Square page mentions "Newark clients" and claims 400+ reviews against the homepage's 700+ ([Maid Sailors](https://maidsailors.com/ny/herald-square-house-cleaning-service/)). Hellamaid shows 4.9 from 428 reviews on one page and 4.8 from 4,000 on another, and an old page still quotes $55/hr and 4.4 from 89 ([reviews](https://hellamaid.ca/reviews/); [New Toronto](https://hellamaid.ca/house-cleaning-service-etobicoke/new-toronto/)). Sparkly Maid's guarantee is 48 hours on one page and 72 on another ([Manhattan](https://www.sparklymaidnyc.com/manhattan); [apartment](https://www.sparklymaidnyc.com/apartment-cleaning)). MaidPro's estimate page is literally titled "MaidPro [location-city]" ([MaidPro](https://www.maidpro.com/estimate/estimate)). **Inconsistency is the most common weakness in the category**, and it is an AEO liability, because an answer engine quotes whichever figure it finds.

**In booking, the local benchmark is Hellamaid.** You enter rooms and type and see the exact price, add extras, pick a slot, and your card is pre-authorised but "only processed after your cleaning has been completed". Changes are free with 24 hours' notice; inside 24 hours there is a $45 fee ([Hellamaid FAQ](https://hellamaid.ca/frequently-asked-questions/)). TIDY adds two refinements: no subscription, and the next available slot shown when today is full ([TIDY same-day](https://www.tidy.com/services/same-day-cleaning)). Homeaglow shows the marketplace weakness: many cleaners approve a request before it is confirmed ([Homeaglow help](https://www.homeaglow.com/help/3)). That is latency an owner-operator doesn't have.

**In service information, three pricing styles compete.** The first is a flat ladder: Sparkly Maid charges $170/$220/$270 by bedroom, a flat +$75 for a deep clean and $60 per add-on, in a guide dated "accurate as of September 2026" ([Sparkly Maid](https://www.sparklymaidnyc.com/blog/apartment-cleaning-cost-nyc)). The second is transparent hourly pricing: Hellamaid charges $59 per cleaner-hour with a 3-hour minimum, plus HST ([Hellamaid](https://hellamaid.ca/house-cleaning-service-toronto/)). The third is an HST-inclusive rate card, and only Custom Maids publishes one locally (condos from $180 for 3 hours) ([Custom Maids](https://www.custommaidstoronto.com/services-costs)). The franchises sell named systems instead of prices, such as The Maids' "22-Step Healthy Touch Deep Cleaning System" ([The Maids](https://www.maids.com/services/)) and MaidPro's "49-Point Checklist" ([MaidPro](https://www.maidpro.com/services/cleaning-schedules)). No Toronto competitor surfaced a room-by-room Regular/Deep/Move comparison, and Clean Proper says it books "by session rather than a fixed checklist" ([Clean Proper](https://cleanproper.ca/services/)). That is open ground.

**The international picture is mostly unverified.** The one confirmed innovation is India's 10–15-minute house help. Snabbit promises vetted help in that window ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/04/25/indias-snabbit-seeks-fresh-funding-at-a-400m-valuation-sources-say/)), Pronto says help arrives "within 15 minutes" and claims 500,000+ homes ([Pronto](https://www.withpronto.com/)), and Urban Company has launched a rival "Insta Help" ([Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/latest/corporate/story/are-snabbit-urban-company-and-pronto-fighting-indias-next-app-war-531140-2026-05-12)). An owner-operator can't keep a standby workforce, but it can sell a premium next-day turnover slot to Airbnb hosts and realtors. Whizz's strongly negative Trustpilot reviews show how marketplace quality swings expose a brand ([Trustpilot](https://ch.trustpilot.com/review/whizz.com.au)), and that weakness flips into the owner-operator's best promise. The platforms the international pass ranked highest from memory alone (Urban Company, Justlife, Hemfrid, Wecasa, Batmaid and Housekeep) are left out of the ranking until a live crawl confirms them. They may displace places seven to ten once it does.

### The craft ceiling sits next door, in robots, pest control and soap

The sites with real design recognition sit just outside the category, and each takes its art direction from one physical truth about what it sells. **Sunday Robotics**, which makes a home-chores robot, was Awwwards Site of the Day on 31 December 2025 at 7.38/10 ([Awwwards](https://www.awwwards.com/sites/sunday)). Its concept, "Playful Precision", uses a palette tuned to the robot's silicone shell, and motion that "mimics the robot's compliant outer shell", kept "subtle and restrained" ([Standard Projects](https://www.standard-projects.com/sunday-robotics/)). It runs on Next.js, Motion and Mux, and the team's stated challenge was "maintaining tier-one performance scores with the sheer volume of video assets" ([Commarts](https://www.commarts.com/webpicks/sunday-robotics)).

**Pest Stop Boys**, a local pest-control firm, is the clearest proof that a small home-service business can win top recognition. It earned Site of the Day plus a Developer Award in February 2020 and CSSDA Website of the Day ([Awwwards](https://awwwards.com/sites/pest-stop-boys); [CSSDA](https://www.cssdesignawards.com/sites/pest-stop-boys/36557/)). The agency Herdl did everything from naming to build, chose "a friendly and engaging experience" over shock tactics, and drew illustrations that scale "both digitally and in the real world" onto print and vans ([Herdl](https://herdl.com/pest-stop-boys/)). Its custom preloader and custom cursor ([One Page Love](https://onepagelove.com/pest-stop-boys)) are the parts not to copy, because both delay or obscure content.

**Blueland**, a cleaning-products brand, colour-codes its packaging by room and puts a half-round symbol, a nod to its tablet with a horizon line, at the centre of its identity ([Blueland](https://www.blueland.com/articles/behind-our-blueland-brand-refresh); [Mindsparkle](https://mindsparklemag.com/designs/blueland)). **Matic**, a floor-cleaning robot, lets visitors "Try virtually": they drop obstacles and watch the robot navigate ([Matic](https://maticrobots.com/)). It is the best idea found for motion that explains the product. **Plus**, a body wash in dissolving packaging, won Site of the Day on a dissolve metaphor ([Awwwards](https://www.awwwards.com/sites/plus)), though the company ceased operations in April 2024 ([CB Insights](https://www.cbinsights.com/company/plus-2)). **Bathhouse Studios**, an NYC venue site by KUDOS, animates its front facade with subtle parallax and large type ([KUDOS](https://www.kudos.nyc/project/bathhouse-studios/)), which is a direct precedent for animating Nest & Rest's landmark art.

The pattern is clear. Sunday's shell becomes its motion, Blueland's tablet becomes its symbol, and Matic's path becomes its interface. Nest & Rest's equivalent truths already exist: the nest (rest), the house (home) and Toronto at night (the hours the client gets back). A site built to Site of the Day standard around them would face almost no competition in its category.

## Rest, night and the owner at the door become the art direction

### Strip the template tells, amplify the assets no one else has

Today's site is careful, accessible and consistent, but its shell reads as a well-made generic template. It has pill buttons, white 14px-radius cards with a 1px border and soft shadow, 44px icons in tinted squares, a rotating discount bar, three numbered steps, a "Questions, answered." accordion on 26 pages, Plus Jakarta Sans with DM Sans, a stock outline icon set, and AI-generated room illustrations disclosed in the footer ([css/site.css](../css/site.css); [index.html](../index.html)). Several patterns were deliberately borrowed from competitors, including Hellamaid's landmark montage beside a quote form ([design notes](../docs/design-notes.md)). A 2026 guide to default AI-tool output lists "purple→blue gradients, a grey 1px border on every card, Inter headlines, three feature cards in a row" and bento grids "reached for by default" ([vibecodekit](https://vibecodekit.dev/ai-slop-design)). Trend writers note that AI has made "clean and competent" look generic ([Bubble](https://bubble.io/blog/web-design-trends/)).

The assets worth amplifying are specific. They are the brand line and its philosophy ("Rest is the real product… We measure ourselves by the hours a family gets back") ([js/config.js](../js/config.js)), and the first-person owner voice: "Hi, I'm Mann. I clean every home myself" and "The person at your door runs the business" ([index.html](../index.html)). They include the code-drawn landmark montage of North York Centre, Mel Lastman Square and the Scarborough Bluffs, whose every colour is a CSS token ([tools/landmarks.mjs](../tools/landmarks.mjs)). And they include the checklist rule that says "Not part of this clean" out loud, plus small rituals: house shoes "especially in a Toronto winter", and door codes deleted within seven days ([js/config.js](../js/config.js)).

The organising idea is a duality that runs through every system. **Clean** is day: crisp, precise, sharp mask reveals, a geometric sans. **Rest** is night: soft, slow, damped "settle" easing, a warm serif. Sunday's "Playful Precision" shows that a two-word duality can carry an entire identity.

### Type that exhales

Replacing the type pairing is the cheapest way to stop looking like a template. Plus Jakarta Sans and DM Sans sit alongside Inter, Geist and Manrope as default startup type, and expressive display serifs paired with quiet sans text are the defining 2026 move ([MadeGoodDesigns](https://madegooddesigns.com/font-trends-2026/)).

The recommended pairing is **Fraunces for display plus Figtree for interface and body**. Both are SIL OFL and free to self-host. Fraunces' opsz+wght axes measure **65.7 KB** for the Latin roman and Figtree **19.7 KB** ([Google Fonts CSS2 API](https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Fraunces); [google/fonts metadata](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl)). That stays well inside a 150 KB font budget. Set "We clean." in Figtree with a crisp mask reveal, and let "You rest." arrive in Fraunces with its weight slowly easing down, an exhale. Push the display size well past today's 84px / weight-700 cap ([css/site.css](../css/site.css)). Fraunces has been popular since 2020, so its distinctiveness has to come from a high SOFT value (rounded, pillowy serifs that suit a nest) at very large sizes. Animating SOFT itself would need the four-axis file at 118 KB.

Two alternatives are worth weighing. Gloock with Mona Sans (about 122 KB) offers a single kinetic word, "rest", relaxing from condensed to wide on Mona's 75–125 width axis. Hedvig Letters, a superfamily drawn for a consumer insurer, was built to signal trust. Whatever the choice, animate variable axes on one short word only. Axis animation re-lays-out text on every frame and cannot run on the compositor.

### A named motion vocabulary replaces fade-ups

Effects you can switch on in Webflow or Framer no longer signal craft: per-character split text on every heading, infinite marquees, magnetic buttons, custom-cursor blobs, glassmorphism, gradient orbs, grain overlays and bento grids. Webflow now hosts GSAP and all its plugins as a site-settings toggle ([Webflow forum](https://discourse.webflow.com/t/webflow-makes-gsap-100-free/319967)), which is why split-text reveals are everywhere. The category's own clichés (bubbles, sparkles, droplets, shields, squeegee wipes) are documented by logo guides ([ManyPixels](https://www.manypixels.co/blog/brand-design/cleaning-logo)).

The research does contain one conflict. The design-led pass proposed a scrolling neighbourhood marquee, while the motion pass lists infinite marquees as a template tell. The tell wins: replace the marquee with a static, linked neighbourhood index or a drawn district map.

The alternative is a small vocabulary with names and rules, every item drawn from rest, home or Toronto.

| Moment | What it means | Where it lives | How to build it | Reduced-motion state |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Settle | Rest: everything lands softly, never bounces | Every entrance and UI response | Three `linear()` easing tokens (about 250 / 600 / 900 ms) shared by CSS and JS | Instant change, feedback kept |
| Kinetic headline | Clean, then rest | Home hero only | Mask reveal on "We clean."; weight exhale on "rest"; the H1 is painted static first | Final static headline |
| Night to dawn | The hours given back | Home story band, district heroes | Layered landmark SVG: windows switch off one by one, sky warms, lake brightens; scroll-driven CSS or one GSAP scrub of at most one viewport | Static dawn frame |
| Clock-aware scene | Personal and local | Home hero | A `data-time` attribute (night or day) set from America/Toronto time; after sunset "You rest." leads | Identical (it is static) |
| Route line | Done for you, room by room | How it works, scope explainer | One SVG stroke draws through a floor plan and closes into the nest mark (DrawSVG, 2.2 KB) | Fully drawn line |
| Build your clean | Motion that converts | Every price box | A small house fills with rooms and ticks as bedrooms, bathrooms and extras change; native inputs, `aria-live` price | Instant fills |
| Card-to-hero morph | Calm continuity | Service card → service page | Cross-document view transitions with a shared `view-transition-name` | Normal page load |
| Fold and tuck | Fresh linen, a made bed | Booking confirmation | CSS 3D `rotateX`, transforms only | Fade |
| One light sweep | Clean, without sparkle | Primary CTA and real photos, once | Animated CSS mask gradient, never looped | None |

The "build your clean" house is the signature. It joins the price box (the site's most distinctive function) and the checklist (its strongest trust device) into a single interaction, on Matic's principle that letting people play with the product explains it. A before/after comparison belongs here too, as a keyboard-operable slider with real photographs, never as a hidden-cursor effect.

### Real photographs and one illustration family

The five room images are AI illustrations, and the wanted list (Mann at work, a deep-clean close-up, real job photos) is still empty ([design notes](../docs/design-notes.md)). Real photography is the single most bespoke asset money can buy here. A roundup cites The Cleaning Authority's homepage video of real employees as a credibility device ([Webcitz](https://www.webcitz.com/blog/best-cleaning-websites/)), and Colorlib calls before/after galleries the most compelling proof of quality ([Colorlib](https://colorlib.com/wp/cleaning-company-websites/)).

The logo SVGs are machine-traced path data of about 21 KB each, and the source artwork isn't in the repository ([assets/logo.svg](../assets/logo.svg)). A clean vector redraw should come first. After that, a once-per-session animation of the house settling into the nest (under 1.2 s, SVG and CSS rather than a 348 KB Rive runtime) gives the brand its signature mark.

Colour should keep the equity it has: navy night and sand window-light stay central, as in the current tokens. Each service then gets its own hue drawn from the Toronto night, in the Blueland colour-coding manner: lake teal, dusk violet, dawn apricot. Every text pairing must be contrast-checked, because today's teal #2FA4A6 manages only 3.01:1 on white ([css/site.css](../css/site.css)). Built as one vector family, the landmarks, nest and room icons can carry onto door hangers, uniforms and car decals the way Pest Stop Boys' illustrations carry onto its vans.

## The hero, booking and service pages sell the price and the scope

### The hero: fix the phone's first screen

At 1440×900 the current hero already fits the 84px headline, the CTA and the whole price box above the fold, and its LCP is the static headline at 460 ms. Phones are where it falls short. At 390×844 the price box starts at 762 px, so its button is off-screen. The landmark art shrinks to a strip under the form. Mobile LCP in a local run was **2,740 ms**, landing on a lede paragraph that runs an entrance animation ([audit measurements](/tmp/claude-0/-home-user-nestnrestcleaning/67aecbd2-25bd-57d0-a71b-323079773156/scratchpad/audit/home-metrics.json)). The repo's own slow-phone lab figure is 2.10 s against a 2.2 s budget ([tools/qa/perf.mjs](../tools/qa/perf.mjs)).

The redesigned hero is one composition at every size. A locality-first H1 ("House cleaning in Scarborough & North York") carries the kinetic display line. Mann's face and first-person lede give the price in the first sentence. A compact "build your clean" control (service, bedrooms, bathrooms, live price) sits inside the first phone screen. The landmark scene becomes a living backdrop behind the type rather than a strip below the form. The title tag carries the differentiator, as Maid Marines does: owner-operated, same cleaner, flat price from $145.

### Booking: re-skin the engine, remove the waits

The booking flow is the most valuable and most tested code in the repository: three steps, 72 named controls, explicit-success-only delivery, retries that keep a reference, and Cal.com and Easy!Appointments hand-offs. A typical request takes **4 typed fields and 5 taps** ([tools/build.mjs](../tools/build.mjs); [js/site.js](../js/site.js)). It should get the new visual language, not a rewrite.

The evidence points to a handful of changes.

The first is to keep the full price visible from the first input to the final review. Baymard finds 48% of abandoners leave over unexpected extra costs, and 16% because they couldn't see or work out the total ([Baymard](https://baymard.com/blog/ux-statistics)).

The second concerns the postal code: check it inside the calculator rather than as a gate, and carry it into the address step. WCAG 2.2 SC 3.3.7 bars making people re-enter information ([Deque](https://dequeuniversity.com/resources/wcag-2.2)).

The third is to collapse "I'm flexible" and "any time" into one choice. Flexible customers currently need two taps to get past date and window.

The fourth is to switch on calendar confirmation once Mann's schedule allows it. Request mode protects a tight schedule, but on Airbnb, instantly bookable listings converted at 3.8% against 2.4% without it (a vendor analysis and an analogy, not cleaning data) ([Rework](https://resources.rework.com/libraries/marketplace-growth/instant-book-vs-request-to-book)). An owner can promise a confirmation from a named person, which a marketplace can't.

The fifth is to keep "pay after the clean" by Interac e-Transfer, a normal choice in Canada: 47% of e-Transfer users have paid a small business with it ([Interac](https://www.interac.ca/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/Interac-SMB-Educational-Resource_Final.pdf)). With no card on file, though, the 24-hour change policy and the late fee of up to 50% must be shown before booking.

The sixth is to validate each field after it is completed, never while the person is typing. In the classic Etre/Wroblewski study, inline validation raised success rates by 22% and cut completion time by 42% ([A List Apart](https://alistapart.com/article/inline-validation-in-web-forms/)). That study is small and dated, but NN/g still recommends the same pattern ([NN/g](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/4-principles-reduce-cognitive-load/)).

The last change concerns motion inside the form, which should confirm cause and effect: a price count of 200–300 ms, a line item sliding into the summary, the "tuck" on success. Nothing should loop, and no chat widget should load (it is already barred from the booking page).

### Service pages: turn the checklist into the signature

Every service page already leads with a price box, then a room-by-room scope (✓ included, "+$" extra, "–" not part of this clean), then a 7-size × 3-home price table ([tools/build.mjs](../tools/build.mjs)). Few sites in the category are this transparent. Its presentation is plain lists, though, and its H1s are bare service names such as "Deep Cleaning".

The rebuild should make four changes. First, give each page an H1 and lede that open with the number: "Deep cleaning in Scarborough & North York from $235, $188 on a first visit". Second, turn the 32-task, four-room checklist in config into an interactive Regular / Deep / Move / Kitchen & Bath scope matrix tied to the "build your clean" house. Third, give that checklist a name (the "Nest & Rest Standard"), since The Maids' 22-Step and MaidPro's 49-Point show that a branded scope signals rigour. Fourth, publish one guarantee wording that is identical on service pages, the booking review and the confirmation email, because Sparkly Maid's 48-versus-72-hour drift is exactly what to avoid. For reference, Molly Maid Canada's benchmark is a free re-clean if a problem is reported within two business days ([Molly Maid](https://mollymaid.ca/?p=25)).

Two expectation-setting pieces cost little. An explicit "what we can't do" list, and a move-out line that promises the checklist rather than the landlord's deposit decision, both cut down on re-clean disputes. Trust badges for insurance, background checks or WSIB should appear only once they are documented. The site's current discipline of leaving the insurance slot commented out until a policy exists is correct, and the fix is to buy the policy.

### Landing pages for every audience and question

The audience pages already exist: Airbnb hosts ("Guests out at 11. Ready for 4."), realtors, gift cards and small offices. Each should get the new art direction and its own Service markup. Airbnb pricing is still "Quoted per unit" because every value in config is null ([js/config.js](../js/config.js)), and publishing real numbers would move it to Hellamaid-level transparency. Two new page types follow from the benchmark. One is a dated "Scarborough & North York house cleaning prices (2026)" guide, the content that Hellamaid, Sparkly Maid and Custom Maids each use to capture "how much does it cost" queries. The other is an honest comparison page, "Owner-operated vs franchise vs marketplace", which is Homeaglow's /vs/ pattern without the attack framing. A premium "next-day turnover" slot for hosts and realtors is the local version of India's instant help.

### Canadian rules draw the lines

Since Bill C-59 (Royal Assent 20 June 2024), advertising a price that can't be paid because of fixed mandatory fees counts as misleading. Only government-imposed charges such as HST are exempt ([Competition Bureau brief](https://sencanada.ca/Content/Sen/Committee/441/NFFN/briefs/SM-C-59_CompetitionBureauofCND_e.pdf)). Enforcement is real: the Federal Court of Appeal upheld the Cineplex ruling, with its record **C$38.9M** penalty, in January 2026 ([Competition Bureau](https://www.canada.ca/en/competition-bureau/news/2026/01/statement-from-the-acting-commissioner-of-competition-on-appeal-courts-ruling-in-cineplex-deceptive-marketing-case.html)). Every animated price, and every machine-readable price, must therefore be the payable amount. Condition-based charges such as heavy pet hair belong inside the instant price, not in a later step.

Ontario's Consumer Protection Act, 2002 s.10 bars charging more than 10% over a written estimate without the customer's agreement ([Ontario](https://www.ontario.ca/page/protecting-consumer-rights-and-safety)). Because the price box says "estimate", the booking review should explain that rule in plain words.

The site says "No HST charged" because `taxRate` is 0. That is lawful only while Nest & Rest is a small supplier at or under $30,000 of taxable revenue; once registered, it must charge HST for at least a year ([CRA](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/gst-hst-account/register-account.html)). The durable selling point is "HST-included totals", which only Custom Maids publishes locally.

Employee or family reviews need disclosure, and businesses can be liable for misleading reviews ([Competition Bureau](https://www.canada.ca/en/competition-bureau/news/2024/01/online-reviews-posted-by-employees-businesses-could-be-liable.html)). Under CASL, a price request gives six months of implied consent for follow-up, and even transactional messages must identify the sender and include an unsubscribe link ([fightspam.gc.ca](https://www.fightspam.gc.ca/eic/site/030.nsf/eng/00008.html)). AODA's website requirement binds only organisations with 50+ employees ([Sencia](https://www.sencia.ca/aoda)), but WCAG 2.2 AA remains the right target for the booking form.

## Answer engines reward static HTML, consistency and reviews

### How engines pick a Toronto cleaner

AI has become a mainstream way to find local businesses. In BrightLocal's 2026 survey, **45% of US consumers** used ChatGPT or another AI tool for local recommendations, up from 6% a year earlier ([BrightLocal](https://www.brightlocal.com/research/local-consumer-review-survey/)). Google says AI Overviews and AI Mode run on its core ranking systems, and coverage of its 2026 guide calls AEO and GEO "still SEO" ([SEJ](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/googles-new-ai-search-guide-calls-aeo-and-geo-still-seo/575026/)). In July 2026, Yelp began licensing its reviews and listings to OpenAI for ChatGPT's local answers ([Axios](https://axios.com/2026/07/23/yelp-reviews-chatgpt-geo-partnership)).

The results are volatile: repeat searches overlap only **20–33%** of the time ([BrightLocal](https://www.brightlocal.com/research/local-ai-visibility-study/)). For Toronto "who is the best" questions in a small study, 8 of 10 AI answers cited a ranking, directory or marketplace, and only 3 of 10 cited a business's own site ([Canada Create](https://canadacreate.com/ai-overviews-2026-toronto-new-york/)). So the website wins price and how-to queries, while reviews and listings win recommendations.

### Write pages the way models lift them

The current site has a structural advantage most animated sites lose: every page is static HTML. Vercel and MERJ found that none of the major AI crawlers (GPTBot, OAI-SearchBot, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot) execute JavaScript. Only Google's and Apple's crawlers render it ([Vercel](https://vercel.com/blog/the-rise-of-the-ai-crawler?ref=t01.li)). The redesign must keep it that way. Prices, headings, neighbourhood names and reviews never go inside canvas, Lottie or video. Split-text spans are generated in the browser only, so the crawled HTML holds clean words. JSON-LD stays inline.

Position on the page matters too. In 18,012 ChatGPT citations, 44.2% came from the first 30% of a page, and 78.4% of question-linked citations came from headings ([ALM Corp summary of Kevin Indig](https://almcorp.com/blog/chatgpt-citations-study-44-percent-first-third-content/)). A separate study of Google AI Overviews found 55% of cited passages in the first 30% ([CXL](https://cxl.com/blog/google-ai-overview-citation-sources/)).

Today the cost guide opens without a number ([tools/guides.mjs](../tools/guides.mjs)). Every service, area and guide page should instead open with a question-style H2 and a self-contained answer built from config. For example: "A regular clean of a one-bedroom, one-bathroom condo in Scarborough or North York costs $145 with Nest & Rest ($116 on a first visit while the founding offer lasts), takes about two hours, includes supplies, and is paid after the clean." The GEO paper found that adding statistics, quotations and cited sources gave 30–40% relative gains on its visibility metric ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2311.09735)). That argues for first-party numbers (average hours by home type across real jobs) and short, quotable tips from Mann.

Freshness should be real, not cosmetic. In Ahrefs' study, URLs cited by AI assistants were 25.7% fresher than organic results ([Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/blog/do-ai-assistants-prefer-to-cite-fresh-content)), so show "Prices reviewed" dates and change them only when the content changes. The deeper moat is that js/config.js is already a single source of truth. If every price, guarantee window and review count is generated from it, the site can't drift the way every competitor's has.

### Schema and crawler policy are plumbing, not levers

The build emits `LocalBusiness`, although the README says `HouseCleaningService`. The business `@id` exists only on the home page, and telephone, street address, geo, `sameAs` and reviews are all absent ([tools/build.mjs](../tools/build.mjs); [README.md](../README.md)). Fix all of these: use `HouseCleaningService`, reference one `@id` from every page, add an `OfferCatalog` whose prices match the visible all-in prices, add `sameAs` to every profile, and keep a `ReserveAction` pointing at /book/.

Expectations should be low. In a controlled Ahrefs study, adding JSON-LD produced "no major uplift in citations on any platform" ([Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/blog/schema-ai-citations/)). Google stopped showing FAQ rich results on 7 May 2026 ([SEJ](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/google-drops-faq-rich-results-from-search/574429/)) and has not shown self-serving review stars for a business on its own site since 2019 ([Google](https://developers.google.com/search/blog/2019/09/making-review-rich-results-more-helpful?authuser=1)).

robots.txt already allows everything. Keep OAI-SearchBot and Claude-SearchBot allowed, because blocking OpenAI's search bot removes a site from ChatGPT search answers ([OpenAI](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/bots)). Don't adopt the new shared `__usage_ai-training` opt-out token: Google treats it as a Google-Extended block, which costs eligibility in Gemini apps ([PPC Land](https://ppc.land/google-microsoft-openai-and-anthropic-back-one-ai-training-opt-out/)). An llms.txt file is optional. About 10% of domains have one, with no measurable citation effect ([PPC Land](https://ppc.land/llms-txt-adoption-rises-8-8x-but-97-of-files-get-zero-ai-requests/)), so if it is added at all it should be generated from config so it never diverges.

### Off-site signals decide recommendations

The config has empty phone, WhatsApp, mailing-address, social and Google-review fields, and the reviews section is hidden because it has no entries ([js/config.js](../js/config.js)). That is the largest GEO gap, and design can't close it. Consumers expect recent proof: 74% look for reviews from the last three months ([PinMeTo summary of BrightLocal](https://www.pinmeto.com/news/brightlocal-local-consumer-review-survey-2026/)).

The fix starts with a complete Google Business Profile under the real business name. Keyword-stuffed names correlate with 2–5x more top-3 placements ([Whitespark](https://whitespark.ca/blog/22-local-seo-developments-you-need-to-know-about-from-q3-2026/)), but they break Google's guidelines and risk suspension. Next comes a phone line that answers, then a steady weekly flow of reviews that mention the service and neighbourhood, each answered personally. After that, claim Yelp, because of its ChatGPT licence, and HomeStars, which a Canadian contractor study found cited by every tracked assistant except ChatGPT ([Northline](https://northlinesystems.ca/resources/why-contractors-need-ai-search-visibility)). Bing Places and Apple Business are free and feed Copilot, Siri and Apple Maps ([AdaTek](https://adatekagency.com/blog/apple-business-connect-now-apple-business/)). Last, pitch "best cleaners in Toronto" roundups and local press: branded web mentions correlate 0.664 with AI Overview visibility, against about 0.218 for backlinks ([Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/blog/ai-overview-brand-correlation/)).

### Rewrite or merge the neighbourhood pages

The 12 neighbourhood pages run 825–968 words, but five-word-shingle similarity between Agincourt and Malvern is **0.72**, and no local job or review renders on any of them ([audit measurements](/tmp/claude-0/-home-user-nestnrestcleaning/67aecbd2-25bd-57d0-a71b-323079773156/scratchpad/audit/pages.jsonl); [js/config.js](../js/config.js)). Google's doorway policy tests usefulness, not a similarity percentage. In case reports, 200+ AI-generated area pages barely moved traffic, while 35 genuinely useful ones at 84% similarity still converted ([AgentSite summary of Sterling Sky](https://agentsite.ai/blog/are-service-area-pages-doorway-pages)).

Each page should carry facts only that place has. Agincourt and Malvern have townhomes, Guildwood and the Bluffs have detached homes, and the condo towers by Scarborough Town Centre and North York Centre have concierge, fob and elevator-booking rules. Add a real job and a neighbourhood review once they exist. Pages that can't earn that substance should merge into the district hub as sections, with 301 redirects. District hubs are where the animated district landmark scenes belong.

### Be agent-ready, and measure honestly

Agentic booking is real but US-first and runs through partners. Google's AI Mode books through platforms such as Booksy and Fresha ([Google](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products/search/search-io-2026/)), and Gemini's calling works only with US numbers as of September 2026 ([iPhone in Canada](https://www.iphoneincanada.ca/2026/09/24/gemini-can-now-make-phone-calls-for-you-canada-has-to-wait/)). Chrome's WebMCP, which lets a site declare actions such as booking to agents, is in an origin trial from Chrome 149 to 156 ([Chrome](https://developer.chrome.com/blog/webmcp-epp)). Treat these as watch items.

What matters today is two things. First, prices and inclusions stated in CAD in HTML. Second, deep-linkable booking URLs: the existing `?s=&h=&bd=&ba=&f=` parameters should gain a postal-code parameter. The booking page also has a real gap: its `<noscript>` message says booking needs JavaScript ([book/index.html](../book/index.html)). A progressively enhanced form with labelled native inputs serves screen readers, browser agents and future WebMCP annotation together.

For measurement, use GA4's native AI Assistant channel (added 13 May 2026, but it excludes Perplexity), plus a custom regex channel group placed above Referral ([Nice Looking Data](https://www.nicelookingdata.com/blog/ga4-ai-traffic-chatgpt-referrals)). Add Search Console's Generative AI report ([Google](https://developers.google.com/search/blog/2026/06/gen-ai-performance-reports)) and Bing's AI Performance report ([Bing](https://blogs.bing.com/webmaster/February-2026/Introducing-AI-Performance-in-Bing-Webmaster-Tools-Public-Preview)). Two low-tech tools complete it: a "How did you find us?" field with ChatGPT and other-AI options, and a monthly panel of about 20 fixed prompts, each run two or three times. Expect single-digit AI referrals at first. Success means being named, and quoted correctly, for district-level price and "best" prompts.

## A 48 KB motion layer on the static engine keeps it fast

### Keep the generator, rebuild the presentation

One settings object renders 38 pages. The browser re-reads the same config to compute prices. About 22 Playwright test files guard pricing parity, legal wording, accessibility and a 2.2 s LCP budget. And the non-technical owner's workflow is "edit config, run build" ([README.md](../README.md)). That contract is worth more than any framework.

The recommended path is to keep the static multi-page site and config.js but rebuild the presentation layer. In practice that means splitting the 199 KB, 2,654-line `build.mjs` into partial templates, replacing its two-regex "minification" with esbuild or lightningcss, shipping the 46 KB config.js minified, moving the 8.8 KB inline icon sprite into a cached file, splitting the single 100 KB stylesheet by page type, and adding AVIF images ([tools/build.mjs](../tools/build.mjs)).

Astro 7 in static mode is a sound alternative if the page count keeps growing. It should use native view transitions rather than its ClientRouter, which forces script re-initialisation that would fight the Cal.com and Chatwoot embeds ([Astro](https://docs.astro.build/en/guides/view-transitions/)). Next.js and SvelteKit add a runtime and SPA routing this site doesn't need.

The platform now carries most of the motion for free. CSS scroll-driven animations became Baseline on 27 October 2026, when Firefox 159 shipped them ([web-features](https://github.com/web-platform-dx/web-features)). Cross-document view transitions run in Chrome/Edge 126+ and Safari 18.2+, and Firefox simply loads the page normally ([Chrome](https://developer.chrome.com/docs/web-platform/view-transitions/cross-document)). Because these are real page loads, focus reset and title announcement work natively. GSAP, including SplitText, ScrollTrigger and DrawSVG, has been free for commercial use since 3.13 on 30 April 2025 ([GSAP](https://gsap.com/blog/3-13/)). It ships under a Webflow licence that can be revised, so pin the version.

| Layer | Choice | Measured cost (gzip) | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| Page transitions | `@view-transition { navigation: auto }` + shared names | 0 KB | Chrome, Edge, Safari animate; Firefox navigates normally |
| Scroll reveals, landmark story | CSS `animation-timeline: view()` / `scroll()` inside `@supports` | 0 KB | Baseline since 27 Oct 2026 |
| UI motion | `popover`/`<dialog>`, `@starting-style`, `linear()` springs, `::details-content` | 0 KB | Baseline |
| Choreography (home, vision) | GSAP 3.15 core + ScrollTrigger + SplitText ([npm](https://www.npmjs.com/package/gsap)) | 48.4 KB | `gsap.matchMedia()` gates reduced motion |
| Light pages | Motion `mini` animate ([npm](https://www.npmjs.com/package/motion)) | 3.3 KB | MIT |
| Landmark and route art | Inline SVG + DrawSVG / MorphSVG | 2.2–9.6 KB | Skip Rive (348 KB WASM) and dotLottie (491 KB WASM) |
| Optional sky shader | OGL fragment shader, lazy, static poster ([npm](https://registry.npmjs.org/ogl)) | 12.8 KB | Never the LCP element; off on Save-Data |
| Smooth scroll | Native | 0 KB | Lenis stops working over iframes such as Cal.com and Chatwoot ([Lenis](https://github.com/darkroomengineering/lenis)) |
| Fonts | Fraunces (opsz+wght) + Figtree, self-hosted, subset | ~85 KB | One preload; total under 150 KB |

### Rules that protect LCP, INP and accessibility

The hard rule is that the LCP heading is painted visible in HTML and is never hidden while waiting for JavaScript. Since Chrome 86, LCP for an element that starts at opacity 0 is measured from when it becomes visible, and since Chrome 130 transparent text can't be the LCP element at all ([Chromium LCP changelog](https://chromium.googlesource.com/chromium/src/+/main/docs/speed/metrics_changelog/lcp.md)). This is exactly how today's mobile entrance animation slows LCP. Animate a decorative layer, or clip-reveal text that is already painted. Animate only transform, opacity and clip-path, and lazy-load any canvas.

The budget is LCP at or under 2.0 s on a mid-range Android over 4G, CLS at or under 0.05 (the repo's current bar), INP at or under 200 ms ([web-vitals](https://github.com/GoogleChrome/web-vitals)), and no more than 100 KB of first-party JavaScript on the home page: today's roughly 45 KB (site script plus config) plus GSAP's 48 KB. No GSAP, WebGL or third-party chat loads on the booking page.

Accessibility needs a few rules of its own. Author all motion inside `prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference`, so the default is static. Add a visible "Pause animations" toggle, because WCAG 2.2.2 requires a pause control for anything that moves for more than five seconds alongside other content ([W3C](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/pause-stop-hide.html)). WCAG 2.3.3 names parallax as a vestibular trigger ([W3C](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/animation-from-interactions.html)). Keep pinned sequences to one viewport or less on the conversion path, and split headings only. The current axe-clean result (0 violations at both widths) and the contrast tests in both themes stay as release gates.

### Move hosting, update the tests, then build in four phases

GitHub Pages' terms say it isn't for running "your online business" or a site "primarily directed at… facilitating commercial transactions" ([GitHub Docs](https://docs.github.com/en/pages/getting-started-with-github-pages/github-pages-limits)). A booking-led business site sits uncomfortably close to that line. Cloudflare Pages or Netlify serve the same static output and allow custom headers. Their free-tier specifics were not verified, and any CDN "block AI bots" toggle must stay off, because it would override the robots.txt intent.

The QA suite encodes the old "calm, not loud" constraints, such as the home page staying within six desktop and ten phone screens. Those tests should be rewritten deliberately, not deleted. Three new checks belong alongside them: reduced-motion screenshots, LCP measured with motion on, and a crawler-view check that the raw HTML contains every price, heading and JSON-LD block.

| Phase | Work | Why this order |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Foundations | Google Business Profile, phone line, review routine, Yelp, HomeStars, Bing Places, Apple Business; schema fixes; dated price guide; answer-first openings | The off-site and content gaps cost more than the design gaps, and reviews take months to build up |
| 2. Identity | Logo redraw, type pairing, colour roles, motion tokens, real photography | Every later screen depends on these |
| 3. Signature pages | Home hero with kinetic type and night-to-dawn story; "build your clean" price-and-scope tool; service pages; view transitions | The highest-intent templates carry the brand |
| 4. Local depth and booking | Rewrite or merge neighbourhood pages using real jobs and reviews; booking re-skin and calendar confirmation; hosting move; measurement panel | Needs the proof gathered in phases 1–3 |

## Conclusion

The design ambition and the AEO strategy turn out to be the same project. The "build your clean" house is at once the site's signature animation and its most quotable content: a price, a scope and a rule anyone can lift in one sentence. Config-as-single-source-of-truth, a dull engineering choice, becomes a competitive moat in a category where every rival contradicts itself across pages. "Highly animated" and "rest" also stop conflicting once motion is defined as slow, damped and meaningful. In a trust-led category with no design leader, restraint executed precisely reads as more premium than spectacle, and it keeps the phone LCP and the AI crawlers' view intact.

The things that will decide whether this works are mostly outside the code. Nest & Rest has no reviews, no listed phone and no linked Business Profile, and AI recommendations lean hardest on exactly those signals, so the review routine should start now, before a single animation ships. The top-ten ranking should also be treated as provisional on craft. The saved crawler scripts should be rerun once competitor hosts can be reached, to score typography, motion and mobile, and to settle whether any of the international platforms belong in places seven to ten.
