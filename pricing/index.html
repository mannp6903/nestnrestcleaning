Questions

Pricing questions.

Anything else? Email nestnrestcleaning@gmail.com.

How is my price calculated?

Your price is based on the service, the number of bedrooms and bathrooms, the type of home and any extras you add. You see it before you book. If your home needs much more or less time than expected, we tell you before we start, never after.

Do I pay when I book?

No. There's no deposit. You pay after the clean by Interac e-Transfer, credit or debit card, or cash.

Should I book a deep clean first?

If your home hasn't had a professional clean in the last few months, yes. A deep clean resets the build-up so every regular visit after it stays quick and consistent.

Are supplies included in the price?

Yes. Every price includes our products and equipment: cloths, mops, a vacuum and cleaning products. There's nothing extra to buy.

Why do townhouses and houses cost more?

A townhouse or house usually has stairs, more floor area and more entrances than a condo with the same number of bedrooms, so it takes longer to clean. The price box adjusts for this automatically.

Do regular plans cost less?

Yes. Visits every week, every 2 weeks or every 4 weeks cost less than a one-time clean, and you can pause or change your plan with 24 hours' notice.
